Lumora Notes Refund Policy

1. Scope

This policy explains when you can get a refund for payments made to Lumora Notes for paid plans and add-ons.

2. New subscriptions

If you are not satisfied with a new paid plan, you can request a full refund within 7 days of your first payment.

3. Annual plans

If you cancel an annual plan after the first 30 days, we refund the unused full months, minus a processing fee of 5%. Monthly plans are not refunded once the billing period has started.

4. How to request a refund

To request a refund, contact support from Settings > Billing and include the email address of your account.

5. Exceptions

We do not refund add-ons that have already been used, such as extra storage or AI credits, or payments for accounts closed because of a breach of the Terms of Service.
